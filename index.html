Ask about a loan

  • Personal loan

    For weddings, travel, medical bills or any personal need. No collateral.

  • Business loan

    Funds to start, run or grow a shop, trading firm or small business.

  • Home loan

    Buy a flat or house, build on your plot, or renovate.

  • Loan against property

    A larger amount for a longer tenure, against a home or shop you own.

  • Balance transfer & top-up

    Move an existing loan to a lower rate, and borrow a little more if needed.

  • Car loan

    New or used car, for personal or commercial use.

  • Two-wheeler loan

    Bikes and scooters, with low down payments.

  • Gold loan

    Quick funds against your gold jewellery, often the same day.

  • Education loan

    Fees and living costs for studies in India or abroad.

  • Working capital & MSME

    Overdraft, cash credit and Mudra-type loans for running costs.

  • Machinery & equipment loan

    Buy machines, tools or medical equipment and pay in EMIs.

  • Loans for professionals

    For doctors, CAs, architects and other professionals to set up or expand practice.
